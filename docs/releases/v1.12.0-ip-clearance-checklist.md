## Material name screening

The overlap of the name **Orchestra** with unrelated products in neighboring AI/coding workflows is a **release-specific risk to evaluate**, not proof of infringement. Current mark status, actual marketplace confusion, commercial use, prior rights, and relevant territories require separate determination. Do **not** unilaterally change the name or classify the brand as cleared. The specific historical U.S. serial is only a lead for authoritative checking; its abandoned status in secondary databases does not resolve every other mark or common-law claim.
