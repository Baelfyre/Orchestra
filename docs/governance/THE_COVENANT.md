# The Covenant

The Covenant is Orchestra's evidence-only cross-governance synthesis layer. It
assesses one exact candidate state after The Steward, The Governor, technical
specialists, and PRAI have produced their own evidence. It does not grant
runtime, transition, merge, release, deployment, policy-activation, or other
protected-action authority. Arbiter remains the transition owner.

## Ownership

The Steward is the PRODUCT_INTENT_SCOPE_AND_SYSTEM_COHERENCE_AUTHORITY. The
Steward judges the verified problem, intended users, Project Context,
ProductIntentContract, project goals, requirements, acceptance criteria,
critical end-to-end flows, project-level invariants, prohibited outcomes,
scope, and complexity/value alignment.

The Governor is the GOVERNANCE_INTEGRITY_AND_PROTECTED_OBLIGATION_AUTHORITY.
The Governor judges applicable legal, regulatory, privacy, compliance,
licensing, IP, policy-integrity, auditability, human-review, protected
authority, and prohibited-governance obligations. Technical mechanisms remain
owned by the relevant specialist.

Clockwork, Cipher, Chronicler, Overseer, Dagger, and other specialists provide
technical or assurance evidence in their own domains. A specialist PASS is not
system coherence. A PRAI PASS is not Covenant PASS.

## Synthesis order

The Covenant evaluates, in order:

1. Prime Directive alignment and explicit human boundaries.
2. Applicable protected obligations.
3. Verified project goals, requirements, and critical flows.
4. Project-level invariant and prohibited-outcome coverage.
5. Cross-specialist contradictions and claim-to-evidence scope.
6. PRAI results, limitations, and durable evidence availability.
7. Steward/Governor conflict and any evidence-backed reconciliation.

It never uses reviewer count, confidence averaging, majority vote, the most
permissive reviewer, or automatic compromise. Precedence is Prime Directive,
explicit human authority, protected obligations, verified project goals,
technical constraints, then schedule or convenience.

## Reconciliation

A conflict may produce RECONCILED_WITH_CONSTRAINTS only when the narrower
design preserves the Prime Directive and verified project goals, both owners
explicitly accept it, and non-empty evidence references support it. A proposal
cannot weaken a constitutional boundary, waive an applicable obligation, or
silently expand accepted scope. A system contradiction remains
REVISION_REQUIRED even when every local review and PRAI are green.

Otherwise the Covenant returns REVISION_REQUIRED, WAIT_FOR_EVIDENCE,
ESCALATE_HUMAN, or BLOCKED according to the governing evidence and ownership.
Unknown legal applicability is escalated to a human. Unknown alignment, stale
state, missing judgments, missing evidence, and unavailable substantive
artifacts fail closed.

## Exact-state binding

Every basis and decision binds to repository, candidate SHA, tree SHA, basis
revision, Project Context, Prime Directive, project goals, critical flows,
system invariants, prohibited outcomes, and authority boundaries. A decision
with a hash of an unavailable artifact is not reproducible assurance. A
material candidate or tree change invalidates the prior result.
