# Worked Example: Governed Analytics Export Change

This is the same joint-governance example packaged with The Governor for progressive disclosure. It is planning-only and does not provide legal advice.

## Verified Facts

- Proposed capability: customer analytics export for an internal analyst role.
- Implementation state: `NOT_STARTED`.
- Runtime or production evidence: `NOT_FOUND`.
- Exact recipients, fields, retention, agreements, and jurisdictions: `PARTIAL_CONTEXT`.

## Source Verification State

- Applicable customer agreements and privacy notices: `SOURCE_NOT_FOUND`.
- Authoritative jurisdiction sources and effective dates: `NOT_VERIFIED`.
- Third-party data or license inventory: `PENDING`.
- Applicability: `NEEDS_HUMAN_INTERPRETATION`.

## Escalation Packet

Operational decision blocked: whether the named fields may be exported to the proposed analyst role.

Required inputs:

1. Product/data owner confirms fields, purpose, users, recipients, retention, and locations.
2. The Steward confirms requirements and change-control scope.
3. The Governor records current authoritative sources and agreements.
4. A qualified privacy/legal owner resolves material applicability and permission questions.
5. Cipher later reviews technical access, minimization, audit, and disclosure controls.

Disposition: `REVISION_REQUIRED`, `human_review_required: true`. No legal conclusion, policy activation, notice publication, implementation authorization, release, or production mutation is made.
